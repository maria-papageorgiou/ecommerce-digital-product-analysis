# E-commerce Digital Product Analysis

## Overview
This project explores e-commerce strategy, digital product positioning, customer engagement, and data-driven decision-making through an Etsy-based digital product business.

The analysis focuses on how digital branding, market research, and user behavior insights can support product optimization and business growth in online marketplaces.

## Objectives
- Analyze customer engagement metrics
- Study digital product positioning
- Explore e-commerce branding strategies
- Apply business analysis principles
- Understand consumer behavior in digital marketplaces

## Tools & Skills
- Canva
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Analysis
- Business Analysis
- Market Research
- E-commerce Strategy

## Key Activities
- Designed printable digital products using Canva
- Conducted competitor and market research
- Analyzed customer interaction metrics
- Evaluated product presentation and branding performance
- Applied analytical thinking to support strategic decisions

## Project Status
Ongoing academic and practical portfolio project.
